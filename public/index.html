Scanned Notice Pipeline

Upload Scanned College Notice to Semantic Chunks

Paper college notices and circulars are scanned into PDF or image files. This pipeline sends the scanned notice to an LLM Vision processor, reconstructs it into structured Markdown with proper headings, timetable tables, and dates, and parses it into semantic chunks with embeddings stored directly in the database.
